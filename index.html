02 — FAQ

AI UGC, avatars and product video
— answered.

What is AI UGC?

Creator-style video made with AI instead of a filmed creator. Same phone-shot look, no casting, no shipping product to creators.

Does AI content actually look real?

Yes, when it's done right. Every video on this page is AI. Our rule: if you can tell, we redo it.

How much does it cost?

It depends on volume and format. Most brands start between $1k and $3k a month; agencies buying volume get custom pricing. Apply and you get a scope and a price within 24 hours.

How fast is delivery?

First concepts in days. Full batches usually land within one to two weeks depending on volume.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Full commercial rights for ads, organic, and paid social, with no licensing fees.

Can the avatar be me or my team?

Yes. We build custom avatars from a short reference recording, so the same face and voice show up every week.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes, white-label. You brief, we produce, you deliver under your name.

What do you need from me to start?

The product or photos of it, your brand notes, and a few reference videos you like. Two minutes to apply, one call at most.
